# Best Friends for Dinner! 🐑🐺

**A cheerful sheep. A wolf who can't hunt. One dinner invitation that gets stranger by the minute.**

Meet Donna and Oz in *Best Friends for Dinner!*, a short kinetic horror visual novel by **Demonward**. The story takes about five minutes to read, but its mix of sweet character art, dark comedy, and uneasy friendship may stay with you longer.

### [▶ Play Best Friends for Dinner! in your browser](https://bestfriendsfordinner.org/)

Our independent game page lets you start the browser edition and explore the story, controls, content warnings, and original artwork. No download is needed to play on the site.
